# What we are trying to achieve

Paste this into the Claude Project instructions. Full engineering context, competitive research, and P0–P2 roadmap: `docs/handover.md`.

---

Sorano is **autonomous growth for indie builders**. A solo founder ships a feature, spends about 40 seconds in the product, and the system turns that update into X posts, a Google-indexable article, UI media, and a check of whether ChatGPT / Perplexity / Claude cite them. It then learns which posts actually performed and writes the next ones better.

We are not building another social scheduler, AI writer, SEO research suite, or CMS. Those already exist (Ahrefs/Semrush for research, Athena/Profound for GEO monitoring, Typefully/Buffer for scheduling). We are collapsing five tools into one closed loop: **capture the update → distribute it → measure it → feed the score back into generation → get cited by AI search**.

**Who it is for:** indie hackers and vibe-coders launching AI-built products. They do not have a growth person. They will not stitch Buffer, Typefully, a changelog CMS, Search Console, and a spreadsheet that tracks “did ChatGPT mention us.”

**How we win (“best” for our ICP):** be the best **autonomous loop for shipping founders**, not the deepest research or enterprise citation tracker. Beat competitors by auto-shipping from product updates and turning GEO gaps into drafts — at Free / $19 / $49.

**The north star:** one X login, one product URL, then continuous organic growth with almost no weekly work. Success looks like: posts go out, articles rank, AI engines cite the product, and the next draft is better than the last because ERI and pgvector reinforcement actually ran.

**Roadmap (detail in handover):**  
- **P0** — Make the sold loop real (wiring, honest UI, ship-feature pack, live GEO, media/replies honesty).  
- **P1** — Deepen the loop (prompt library, gap→action, light share of voice, visible ERI learning).  
- **P2** — Category leadership only after P0–P1 (citation sources, optional AI Overviews, stronger media/replies).  

**Anti-goals until the loop is honest:** mini-Ahrefs, multi-network social, teams/agencies, LinkedIn, chasing 10 AI engines.
